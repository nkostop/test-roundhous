Hello, world

April 12, 2026

This is the obligatory first post. I'm starting a small notebook here, mostly to give myself a place to write down the things I learn — and to make sure I actually do learn them, instead of just reading and moving on.

I've been a programmer for long enough to know that the ideas I don't write down are the ones I lose. So this is a stake in the ground. I'd rather have a messy public notebook than a clean private one I never read.

The plan is short posts, written quickly, mostly about software: tools I'm using, bugs I've chased, designs I'm thinking about. The bar for publishing is "would I find this useful six months from now?" — not "is this brilliant?"

If something here helps you too, that's a happy accident. If you spot something wrong, please tell me — the email is in the footer.

Thanks for reading. More soon.
