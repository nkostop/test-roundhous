About me

I'm Nikos Kostopoulos — I write software and post the occasional note here.

What I work on

Most of my time goes into building developer tools and small platforms — the kind of focused, low-ceremony software that helps engineers move a little faster without getting in their way. I like projects with sharp scope, honest defaults, and not much magic. When something can be a static file instead of a service, I'll usually pick the static file.

Help Center

A few common questions. Can't find what you need? Reach me via the contact page.

How often do you publish?

Whenever I have something worth writing down — usually a few short notes per month, not on a fixed schedule.

Can I republish or quote your posts?

Yes, with attribution and a link back to the original post. For longer excerpts, please email me first.

Do you accept guest posts?

Not at the moment — this is a personal notebook, so all posts are written by me.

Is there an RSS feed?

An RSS feed is on the roadmap (linked as feed.xml in the footer). It's a placeholder for now.

How can I get in touch?

The fastest way is the form on the contact page, or email me directly at nikos@papergo.io.
