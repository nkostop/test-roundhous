Notes on writing more, editing less

April 28, 2026

My problem with writing has never been writing. It's been editing. I draft something, get bored of it, decide it isn't sharp enough, and shelve it. Repeat until the year is over and nothing got published.

So this month I tried something embarrassing: I gave myself permission to post things that are fine. Not great, not finished — just fine. The rule was one read-through and a spell-check, and then it goes up.

The first few felt awful. By the fourth one, I noticed I was thinking about the topic more carefully while drafting, because I knew the draft was the thing. That shift alone was worth the price of admission.

The other surprise: nobody minded. The posts I was most embarrassed by got the kindest responses. Turns out a half-formed idea, shared in good faith, is more interesting than a perfect one nobody ever sees.

I'll keep doing this. If you've been sitting on a draft, consider this a gentle nudge.
